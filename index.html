PCB Design · Power Electronics · Motor Control

FOC BLDC Motor Driver — 48V / 30A

Designed a 3-phase FOC gate-driver board for BLDC motor control, rated at 48 V input and 30 A continuous output. Features a 6-MOSFET half-bridge topology (Q1–Q6), dedicated gate-driver IC, bulk capacitor bank for ripple filtering, and hardware over-current protection. High-current copper pours optimized for thermal management.

Designed and developed an autonomous mobile robot for indoor logistics within an Industry 4.0 environment. Deployed a distributed ROS2 system on Raspberry Pi with RPLiDAR and SLAM for real-time mapping and navigation. Developed embedded C/C++ firmware for motor control and TCP/IP communication.

PCB Design · STM32 · CAN Bus

STM32 Dual-Channel CAN Bus Gateway

4-layer PCB gateway built around the STM32F407VGT7 (ARM Cortex-M4, 1 MB Flash) enabling simultaneous monitoring and bridging of two independent CAN networks via USB Type-C. Features dual SN65HVD230M-EP industrial transceivers, impedance-controlled differential pair routing (90 Ω USB, 120 Ω CAN), split 5V/3.3V power plane architecture, and a 16-pin expansion header exposing ADC, PWM, and GPIO. Full multi-sheet hierarchical schematic in Altium Designer with DRC compliance targeting JLCPCB manufacturing standards. Applicable to automotive diagnostics, industrial automation, and embedded development.
